# First Cloud Journey (FCJ) Internship Feedback

## English Version

### 1. Working Environment
The working environment is positive, friendly, and not overly rigid. The Admin team members are always ready to help whenever I have questions, even outside of official working hours. The workspace is comfortable and pleasant.

### 2. Support from Mentor and Admin
Mentors provide detailed, enthusiastic guidance and constantly encourage us to ask questions. Their response time is remarkably fast and thorough. Additionally, the intern management department provides thoughtful support with paperwork and administrative procedures, allowing me to fully focus on my work.

### 3. Relevance to Academic Major
The assigned tasks align closely with the foundational knowledge I learned at university, while also opening up opportunities to acquire new, specialized knowledge.

### 4. Learning & Skill Development
I have developed many critical skills, such as project management, teamwork, professional communication, and handling tasks within a corporate environment. The Mentors and Admins also frequently share valuable real-world experiences, making it much easier to visualize my future career path.

### 5. Company Culture & Team Spirit
The company culture is positive and welcoming: everyone respects one another, works seriously, yet maintains a cheerful atmosphere. When facing tight project deadlines, everyone supports each other, making me feel like a true member of the team despite being just an intern.

### 6. Internship Policies & Benefits
The company provides an internship allowance and offers a flexible working hours policy. Furthermore, being able to participate in internal training sessions has been a highly valuable opportunity to learn beyond the scope of my daily tasks.

### 7. What were you most satisfied with during your internship?
- **Most satisfying aspect:** The dedicated guidance from my mentor and the supportive spirit of all team members.
- **Areas for improvement:** Organize more team-building activities and workshops to further enhance the internship experience.
- **Recommendation to friends:** I will definitely recommend this program because of its professional environment and abundant learning opportunities. This is the perfect environment for anyone looking to gain practical, hands-on experience rather than just theoretical knowledge.

### Suggestions & Expectations
- A clearer and more refreshing onboarding roadmap could be developed.
- I would love to continue participating in the program if the opportunity arises.
- The program delivers a high-quality internship experience and offers clear career orientation.
